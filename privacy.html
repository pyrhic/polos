Privacy Policy

wizard-yt · Last updated: October 23, 2026

wizard-yt is a personal tool that prepares short videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned by its operator. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

  • We request only the YouTube scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets the app upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose when you sign in.
  • We do not read your YouTube watch history, subscriptions, comments, analytics, email, contacts or any other Google account data.

How we use and store it

  • The sign-in token is used only to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, that the operator prepared and chose to upload.
  • The token is stored as an encrypted secret in the operator's private GitHub repository and is never displayed in the app or saved in its public database.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the stored token deleted, email us and we will remove it within 7 days.
